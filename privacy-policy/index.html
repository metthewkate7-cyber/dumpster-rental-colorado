Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we collect when you use this site, how we use it, and how you can ask us to delete it. It is written in plain words. Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Home › Privacy Policy

What we collect

We collect only what you give us. The booking form collects your name, phone number, city, street address, delivery date, rental length, dumpster size, placement, and any notes you add. The contact form collects your name, phone number, city, and message. That is the full list.

We do not collect payment details on this site. No payment is taken on the booking page. Payment happens only after we confirm your exact price by phone. We do not ask for your date of birth, your Social Security number, or any other sensitive personal detail.

How we use your information

We use your details for one purpose: to arrange your dumpster rental. That means confirming your size, dates, and price by phone, scheduling delivery and pickup, and answering your questions. We do not use your details for any other purpose.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it with marketers. We do not send your details to advertisers. If a delivery partner needs your address to bring the dumpster, we share only the address and the placement notes for that job.

Cookies and site data

This site may use basic cookies for security and performance. The hosting platform may log visits in the usual way: pages viewed, browser type, and rough location by city. These logs help us keep the site fast and safe. They do not identify you by name.

We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site. We do not build advertising profiles from your visits.

How long we keep your information

We keep booking and contact details only as long as needed to complete your rental and answer follow-up questions. After that, we delete them on request. We do not keep a marketing list. If you never book, your contact form details are not used for anything else.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Use the contact form or call [PHONE] and say you want your details deleted. We will delete the name, phone number, address, and messages you sent us, and confirm the deletion with you.

You can also ask what information we hold about you. Send the same request through the contact form or by phone. We will tell you what we have and delete it if you ask.

Children

This site is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to book for you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. The version on this page is the one that applies. If you have questions about this policy, use the contact form or call [PHONE].
