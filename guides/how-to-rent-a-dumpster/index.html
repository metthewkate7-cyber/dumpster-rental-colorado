How to Rent a Dumpster in Colorado

How to rent a dumpster in Colorado is a six step process: pick a size, pick your dates, book, prepare the spot, load it, and schedule pickup. This guide walks through each step in order.

Quick answer

Renting a dumpster takes six steps. Pick your size, pick your dates, book, prepare the spot, load the box level, and call for pickup. Most rentals run 7 days. You do not need to be home for delivery or pickup.

Step 1: pick your size

Pick the box that fits your job. A 10 yard box fits a small cleanout. A 20 yard box fits a kitchen remodel or a multi room cleanout. A 30 yard box fits a whole house cleanout. Heavy debris needs a 10 yard box.

Use our size guide and the size estimator to choose. When you are between two sizes, size up. The price gap is small. A second haul costs far more.

Step 2: pick your dates

Pick a delivery date and a rental length. Most rentals run 7 days. The clock starts on delivery day. Book 2 to 3 days ahead in busy seasons. Morning calls give the best shot at next day delivery.

Think about your work pace. A garage cleanout may need 3 days. A kitchen remodel may need 10. Pick a length that fits the job, not the shortest term. An extension costs $10 to $25 per day in most markets. Our rental length guide covers the term and the extension cost.

Step 3: book your dumpster

Book online or by phone. Share your city, size, debris type, and dates. Ask for the total in writing. The quote should list the base price, included tons, overage rate, and extra day rate.

Ask about the permit if the box will sit on the street. Ask about boards for driveway protection. Ask what is banned from the load. A short call now saves a surprise fee later. Our cost guide lists every fee to ask about.

Step 4: prepare the spot

Clear a flat spot for the box. Move cars and bins. Lay plywood under the rails to protect the drive. Check overhead wires and tree limbs. The truck needs 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance.

Mark the spot with cones or chalk so the driver can see it. Share gate codes with the dispatcher if your home is gated. A blocked drive on delivery day can mean a failed trip and a $50 to $150 trip fee.

Step 5: load the dumpster

Load heavy items first and flat. Break down furniture. Keep the load level and below the fill line. Keep banned items out. Bag loose trash so it does not blow away.

Do not pile debris above the rim. The driver cannot haul an overfilled box. Our overfill guide shows the fill line rule and the cost of breaking it. Our allowed items guide lists what can and cannot go in.

Step 6: schedule pickup

Call when the box is full or your rental ends. Most pickups happen within 1 to 3 business days. Keep the area clear for the truck. You do not need to be home for pickup.

Call at least 24 hours before you want the box gone. If you need more time, ask for an extension on the same call. The daily rate is often $10 to $25. If the box is full early, ask for a swap. A fresh box keeps the job moving.

How to rent a dumpster without hidden fees

The trick is to ask for the full price before you book. Ask for the base price, the included tons, the overage rate, the extra day rate, and the trip fee. Ask whether delivery, pickup, tax, and disposal are all in the number. Ask about mattress, tire, and appliance fees.

Get the answers in writing. A written quote lists every charge. If the final bill differs, the written quote is your proof. Keep it until the bill is paid and the box is gone.

Before you book, run this checklist. Size picked. Dates picked. Spot cleared and marked. Boards laid. Gate code shared. Banned items set aside. Permit in hand if the box sits on the street. Each item takes a minute. Together they stop most surprise fees.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home for delivery?

No. You do not need to be home if the spot is marked and clear. Mark the spot with cones or chalk. Share gate codes with the dispatcher if your home is gated.

How far ahead should I book?

Book 2 to 3 days ahead in busy seasons. Spring and summer fill fast. A morning call gives the best shot at next day or same day delivery.

What do I need to book a dumpster?

Your name, phone, delivery address, city, size, debris type, and dates. Have your payment method ready. No payment is taken until your exact price is confirmed.

Can I change my delivery date after booking?

Yes, in most cases. Call as early as you can. A date change the day before delivery is usually free. A change after the truck is loaded may add a trip fee.

What if the dumpster arrives with debris already in it?

Photograph it at once. Tell the hauler before you load anything. Photo proof shows the debris was not yours, so you are not charged for its weight.
