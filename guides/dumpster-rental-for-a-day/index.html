How Much to Rent a Dumpster for a Day in Colorado

How much to rent a dumpster for a day is less than most renters expect to save, because delivery and disposal cost the same for one day or seven. A one day rental often lands near the standard 7 day price.

Quick answer

A one day rental is possible, but it rarely costs much less than a 7 day rental. Delivery, pickup, and disposal dominate the bill. A 20 yard box runs about $330 to $500 for a day rental and about the same for a week. A weekend rental is often the smarter short term.

How much to rent a dumpster for a day?

A one day rental is possible in most Colorado cities. The price is not one seventh of a weekly rental. Most of the cost is delivery, pickup, and disposal. Those costs stay the same whether you keep the box one day or seven days.

Published market ranges put a 10 yard box near $300 to $450 and a 20 yard box near $330 to $500 for a standard term. A one day rental often lands near the same range. The saving for a short term is small. Ask your hauler for the one day total and the 7 day total side by side.

Why a day rental costs almost as much as a week

Three costs do not shrink with time. The truck drive to your home costs the same. The landfill tip fee costs the same. The driver time for drop off and pickup costs the same. Only the daily rental fee shrinks, and it is the smallest part of the bill.

Some haulers charge a 3 day minimum. A 3 day term covers the truck cost and a short rental window. A true 1 day rental exists, but it is rare. The hauler must fit two truck trips into one day. That is hard on a busy route.

When a day rental makes sense

A day rental fits a fast, ready job. A crew tears off a roof in one day. A cleanout crew loads a box in one afternoon. A demolition crew fills a box before lunch. In each case, the load is ready and the crew is on site.

It also fits a tight deadline. A home sale closing, a move out day, or an HOA deadline can force a one day window. If the load is sorted and staged before delivery, one day is enough.

It does not fit a slow sort. An estate cleanout takes days of sorting. A remodel produces debris over a week. A garage cleanout with no help takes a weekend. Pick a 7 day term for those jobs. Our rental length guide covers the standard terms.

How to make a one day rental work

Stage the load before delivery day. Pile debris near the drop spot. Break down furniture. Bag loose trash. A staged load goes into the box in hours, not days.

Book early. Call the day before, or the morning of delivery. Same day delivery can add $25 to $75. Morning calls get the best slots. Tell the hauler the box must be picked up the same day or the next morning.

Keep the crew on site. A one day rental with no crew is a gamble. If the load is not ready, the box sits and the day is lost. Have help lined up before the truck arrives.

Weekend rentals: the smarter short term

A weekend rental is often smarter than a one day rental. Friday delivery and Monday pickup gives you two full work days. The price is close to a 3 day term. Most haulers offer this pattern.

Book the weekend early in the week. Friday slots fill fast in spring and summer. Mark the spot and lay boards before Friday. Load on Saturday, finish on Sunday, and call for Monday pickup. Our how to rent guide walks through each step in order.

What to ask before a day rental

Ask five questions before you book a day rental. What is the one day total? What is the 7 day total? What time will the truck arrive? What time must the box be ready for pickup? What is the fee if the pickup slips to the next day?

Ask about the minimum term too. Some haulers set a 3 day minimum even when they advertise a day rental. Ask whether same day delivery adds a fee. Some haulers add $25 to $75 for a same day slot.

Get the answers in writing. A day rental leaves no room for a surprise. The truck comes, the box fills, and the truck leaves. Every fee must be known before the first trip.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rent a dumpster for just one day?

Yes, in most markets. A one day rental is possible. You still pay the delivery and pickup cost. The daily rate is not the full price.

Is a one day rental cheaper than a 7 day rental?

Rarely by much. Most of the price is delivery, pickup, and disposal. Those costs do not shrink with a shorter term. A 7 day rental often costs little more than a 1 day rental.

Can I rent a dumpster for a weekend?

Yes. A weekend rental is a common pick. Book it early in the week. Friday delivery and Monday pickup is a standard weekend pattern.

What is the minimum rental period?

Many haulers set a 3 day minimum. Some offer a true 1 day rental. Ask the minimum term when you call. It is in the quote.

Do I pay extra for same day delivery?

Sometimes. Same day delivery can add $25 to $75. Call before mid morning for the best shot at a same day slot.
