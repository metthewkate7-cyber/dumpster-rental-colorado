Durango local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Durango is the county seat of La Plata County, with 19,071 residents at the 2020 Census. ZIP codes are 81301, 81302, and 81303. Main areas are downtown, North Main, Florida Road, Bodo Park, Three Springs, and the Animas Valley. Fort Lewis College drives student turnover each spring and fall. Many homes are older Victorians and brick houses with tight alleys and short driveways.

Permits follow the universal rule in Durango. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street or in a public right of way needs city approval first. Check with the City of Durango Public Works department before you place a street dumpster. Fees and lead times change, so confirm them direct. Do not block a sidewalk or a bike lane.

Debris from Durango loads goes to the City of Durango Transfer Station at 790 Tech Center Drive, just west of town on US Highway 160. The Bondad Landfill sits south of town for larger loads. Paint, oil, batteries, and electronics never go in a dumpster. Take those to the county household hazardous waste program instead. Many Durango homes sit on septic systems, so keep the truck off leach fields and soft ground.
