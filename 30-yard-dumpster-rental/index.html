What a 30 yard dumpster rental holds

A 30-yard bin holds about 170 to 190 trash bags. That is 9 full pickup truck loads. It is the size you want when the job covers a whole house or a big renovation.

Furniture, drywall, flooring, cabinets, and old fixtures all fit together. The 6-foot walls hold bulky waste that would spill out of a low bin. You load from the side door or toss over the top.

One 30-yard haul usually costs less than two 20-yard hauls. You pay one delivery fee and one pickup fee. That is why contractors pick this size for big jobs.

The bin is 6 feet tall, so loading by hand over the side is hard. Use the walk-in door at the back. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance, plus 25 to 30 feet of vertical clearance for the lift.

Weight allowance and overage fees

Most 30-yard rentals include 3 to 5 tons of debris. That covers a whole house of household junk and light building waste. Mixed construction debris can push past the limit, so watch what goes in.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. Dumpster Rental Dogs charges $85 per ton over the limit in Colorado Springs. In Denver, it is about $95 per ton. HBS Trash in Denver charges $75 per ton. Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled and may draw an extra fee.

Never fill a 30-yard bin with concrete, dirt, or brick. Heavy material hits the 3 to 5 ton limit at a small fraction of the volume. A 10-yard dumpster is the right bin for heavy debris.

Best jobs for a 30-yard dumpster

  • Whole-house cleanout, every room at once
  • Major renovation with framing, drywall, and flooring
  • Large construction waste from a build or remodel
  • Estate cleanout of a large home
  • Roofing tear-off on a large home
  • Hoarding cleanout with years of stored items
  • Commercial cleanout of an office or retail space

For demolition waste, the 40-yard dumpster holds even more bulky debris. For a multi-room job, the 20-yard dumpster costs less.

When a 30-yard dumpster is the wrong size

A 30-yard bin is wrong for heavy debris. Concrete, dirt, brick, and tile weigh too much for a bin this tall. You would hit the weight limit with the bin a quarter full. Use a 10-yard bin for heavy loads.

It is also wrong for a small job. A single-room cleanout in a 30-yard bin wastes money and driveway space. The 10-yard dumpster or 15-yard dumpster fits small jobs better.

And it is wrong for a tight driveway. The bin is 22 feet long and 6 feet tall. If the truck cannot reach the spot, or the bin blocks the garage, a smaller bin or a street spot with a permit is the better call.

Where a 30-yard dumpster fits

A 30-yard bin needs a long, clear driveway or a street spot. At 22 feet long and 6 feet tall, it blocks sight lines and takes most of a two-car drive. Plan parking before delivery day.

Put plywood boards under the rails. A full 30-yard bin is heavy, and boards spread the load. They protect concrete from cracks and asphalt from ruts.

Street placement may need a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. In Pueblo, street placement goes through the Pueblo Transportation Division. Check the Colorado permit guide for your city.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 30-yard dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges for a 30-yard bin run $345 to $895. A typical local price is $500 to $650. The national average was $809 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How many trash bags fit in a 30-yard dumpster?

About 170 to 190 standard trash bags fit in a 30-yard dumpster. That is about 9 pickup truck loads. Break down furniture and flatten boxes to fit more in.

Is a 30-yard dumpster big enough for a whole-house cleanout?

Yes, for most homes. A 30-yard bin is the standard pick for a whole-house cleanout, including furniture, boxes, and household junk from every room. A very large home or a hoarding cleanout may need a 40-yard bin.

Can I put concrete in a 30-yard dumpster?

No, not as a full load. Concrete, dirt, and brick hit the 3 to 5 ton weight limit when the bin is only part full. Use a 10-yard dumpster for heavy debris and fill it only part way.

Will a 30-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

It fits most long driveways, but it takes the full length. The bin is about 22 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 6 feet tall. Measure first, and plan where your cars will park during the rental.

How long can I keep a 30-yard dumpster?

The usual rental runs 7 to 14 days, and 7 days is the most common choice. Extra days typically run $10 to $25 per day. Choose your rental length at booking and extend early if the job runs long.
