What size fits your job in Greeley?

Greeley jobs split three ways: student move-outs, farm and barn cleanouts, and construction debris. Student move-outs take a 10-yard dumpster. Barn cleanouts take a 20-yard or 30-yard dumpster. Construction debris takes a 30-yard dumpster. Pick the size that fits both the job and the lot.

  • 10-yard dumpster: small cleanout, one room, student move-out, or heavy debris like concrete and dirt. About 3 pickup loads. About 50 to 70 bags.
  • 15-yard dumpster: mid-size cleanout, two-room remodel, or flooring through a house. About 4 to 5 pickup loads. About 80 to 100 bags.
  • 20-yard dumpster: the most rented size in Greeley. Kitchen remodel, multi-room cleanout, barn cleanout, or roofing tear-off. About 6 pickup loads. About 110 to 130 bags.
  • 30-yard dumpster: whole-house cleanout, major renovation, or large construction debris. About 9 pickup loads. About 170 to 190 bags. Not for heavy debris.
  • 40-yard dumpster: major demolition or large light bulky debris. About 12 pickup loads. About 230 to 250 bags. Never for concrete, dirt, or brick.

Book on the booking page in three steps. Choose a size. Choose a delivery date and rental days. Leave your address and phone number. We call to confirm your size, dates, and exact price. You pay nothing on the booking page.

Delivery day is simple. Clear the driveway spot before the truck arrives. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. Loading rules keep you safe from extra fees. Fill the dumpster level with the top edge, never above it. Keep heavy material at the bottom and light material on top.
