Estes Park local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Estes Park is a town in Larimer County, with 5,904 residents at the 2020 Census. The ZIP code is 80517. Main areas are downtown Elkhorn Avenue, the Fall River corridor, and Marys Lake. Homes mix historic lodges, cabins, and mountain homes. The town sits at 7,522 feet at the east entrance to Rocky Mountain National Park.

Permits follow the universal rule in Estes Park. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street or in a public right of way needs town approval first. Check with the Town of Estes Park Public Works department before you place a street dumpster. Downtown streets are busy with visitors in summer.

Debris from Estes Park loads goes to the Estes Park Transfer Station at 455 Elm Road, run by Waste Management. Larger loads go to the Larimer County Central Diversion and Transfer Station at 2388 West Trilby Road in Fort Collins, and on to the new Larimer County North Landfill near Wellington. Paint, oil, and batteries never go in a dumpster. Estes Park sits in elk and bear country, so never leave food waste in an open dumpster overnight.
