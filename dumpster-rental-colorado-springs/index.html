What size fits your job in Colorado Springs?

Most Colorado Springs jobs need a 10-yard, 20-yard, or 30-yard dumpster. Small jobs take the 10-yard. Most remodels take the 20-yard. Whole-house cleanouts take the 30-yard. Use the size guide below, then check your size with the free estimator.

  • 10-yard dumpster: small cleanout, one room, small bathroom, or heavy debris like concrete and dirt. About 3 pickup loads. About 50 to 70 bags.
  • 15-yard dumpster: mid-size cleanout, two-room remodel, or new flooring through a house. About 4 to 5 pickup loads. About 80 to 100 bags.
  • 20-yard dumpster: the most rented size in Colorado Springs. Kitchen remodel, multi-room cleanout, flooring, or roofing tear-off. About 6 pickup loads. About 110 to 130 bags.
  • 30-yard dumpster: whole-house cleanout, major renovation, or large construction debris. About 9 pickup loads. About 170 to 190 bags. Not for heavy debris.
  • 40-yard dumpster: major demolition or large light bulky debris. About 12 pickup loads. About 230 to 250 bags. Never for concrete, dirt, or brick.

Still picking a size? Use the dumpster size calculator. It asks about your job in Briargate, the Powers corridor, or Falcon. Then it points to the right size in under a minute. Compare sizes in the full Colorado prices guide too.

Heavy debris changes the choice. Concrete, dirt, brick, and tile are heavy. They fill a small dumpster by weight long before they fill it by space. A 10-yard dumpster is the right call for a concrete patio tear-out in Briargate or a dirt job in Falcon. A 30-yard or 40-yard dumpster full of concrete would break the weight limit and cost you a big overage fee. Read the weight limits guide before you load heavy material.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster on my driveway in Colorado Springs?

No. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no city permit in Colorado Springs. A permit is only needed when the dumpster sits on a public street or sidewalk. That is a Land Use Review revocable permit from the City of Colorado Springs, phone 719-385-5905.

What size dumpster fits a kitchen remodel in Colorado Springs?

A 20-yard dumpster fits most kitchen remodels in Colorado Springs. It holds about 6 pickup loads and 110 to 130 bags. It takes cabinets, flooring, drywall, and old fixtures. Pick a 10-yard dumpster instead if you only tear out a small kitchen with light debris.

How much does a 20-yard dumpster cost in Colorado Springs?

Published market prices put a 20-yard dumpster near $350 for a short rental in Colorado Springs. The source is All American Dumpster, checked November 14, 2026. The national average for a 20-yard dumpster was $679 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Your exact price is confirmed when you book.

Will a dumpster damage my driveway in Colorado Springs?

A dumpster can scuff or crack a weak driveway. Boards go under the rails on every driveway drop in Colorado Springs. Concrete driveways in Briargate and the Powers corridor hold up well with boards. Older asphalt in Old Colorado City needs extra care, so tell us about cracks before delivery.

Can I put a dumpster on the street in Old Colorado City or Manitou Springs?

Yes, but only with a Land Use Review revocable permit from the City of Colorado Springs, phone 719-385-5905. Streets in Old Colorado City are narrow. Manitou Springs has its own town rules. Check with the town before you place a dumpster there. Keep the driveway as your first choice.

What items can I not put in a dumpster in Colorado Springs?

You cannot load paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, appliances with freon, or hazardous waste. El Paso County runs household hazardous waste drop-off days for those items. Ask us before you load anything wet, heavy, or odd, and we will point you to the right place.
