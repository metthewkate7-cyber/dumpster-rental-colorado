40 Yard Dumpster Rental in Colorado

A 40 yard dumpster rental is the largest roll-off size we offer in Colorado. It holds about twelve pickup loads of light, bulky debris from demolition and large builds.

Quick answer

A 40-yard dumpster holds about 12 pickup loads, or 230 to 250 trash bags. It measures about 22 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 7 to 8 feet high. It suits major demolition, large construction jobs, and light bulky debris only. The national average price was $906 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com.

What a 40 yard dumpster rental holds

A 40-yard bin holds about 230 to 250 trash bags. That is 12 full pickup truck loads. It is the biggest bin on a standard roll-off truck, built for jobs that produce a mountain of light waste.

Demolition debris fits well: torn-out walls, wood framing, roofing, siding, and flooring. The 7 to 8 foot walls hold bulky pieces that would overflow a shorter bin. You load through the walk-in door at the back.

One 40-yard haul beats two smaller hauls on a big job. You save a delivery fee and a pickup fee, and the crew keeps working instead of waiting on a swap.

The bin is 7 to 8 feet tall, so you cannot toss waste over the side by hand. Use the walk-in door. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance, plus 25 to 30 feet of vertical clearance for the lift.

Weight allowance and overage fees

Most 40-yard rentals include 4 to 6 tons of debris. That sounds like a lot, but light bulky waste fills the volume long before it hits the weight limit. That is the point of this size: volume, not weight.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled safely and may draw an extra fee.

Never fill a 40-yard bin with concrete, dirt, brick, or tile. Heavy material hits the 4 to 6 ton limit when the bin is barely a quarter full. A 10-yard dumpster is the right bin for heavy debris.

Best jobs for a 40-yard dumpster

  • Full demolition of a house or large structure
  • Major construction waste from a large build
  • Whole commercial cleanout, office or retail
  • Large roofing tear-off with bulky waste
  • Multi-unit cleanout, apartments or rentals
  • Large estate cleanout with furniture in every room
  • Storm cleanup with branches and light debris

For a whole-house cleanout that is not demolition, the 30-yard dumpster usually costs less. For a kitchen remodel, the 20-yard dumpster is the standard pick.

When a 40-yard dumpster is the wrong size

A 40-yard bin is wrong for heavy debris, full stop. Concrete, dirt, and brick hit the weight limit fast in a bin this big. You would pay for a huge bin and use a quarter of it. Use a 10-yard bin for heavy loads.

It is also wrong for a small or mid-size job. A 40-yard bin costs the most and needs the most space. A single-room cleanout in this bin wastes money. The 10-yard dumpster or 15-yard dumpster fits small jobs better.

And it is wrong for a tight site. The bin is 22 feet long and up to 8 feet tall. It blocks sight lines, needs a long clear drop zone, and the truck needs full clearance to lift it. A smaller bin or a street spot with a permit is the better call.

Where a 40-yard dumpster fits

A 40-yard bin needs a long, clear space. A jobsite, a large driveway, or a permitted street spot works. At 22 feet long and up to 8 feet tall, it takes a big footprint and blocks views from the street.

Put plywood boards under the rails. A full 40-yard bin is the heaviest load a roll-off truck carries, and boards spread that weight. They protect concrete from cracks and asphalt from ruts.

Street placement may need a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. In Fort Collins, a Permit to Obstruct costs $25. Check the Colorado permit guide for your city.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 40-yard dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges for a 40-yard bin run $550 to $995. A typical local price is $650 to $820. The national average was $906 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How many trash bags fit in a 40-yard dumpster?

About 230 to 250 standard trash bags fit in a 40-yard dumpster. That is about 12 pickup truck loads. Break down large items and load through the walk-in door to fit more in.

Is a 40-yard dumpster good for demolition?

Yes, it is the standard pick for major demolition. Torn-out walls, wood framing, roofing, and siding are light and bulky, which is exactly what a 40-yard bin is built for. Never load it with concrete or dirt.

Can I put concrete in a 40-yard dumpster?

No. Concrete, dirt, and brick hit the 4 to 6 ton weight limit when the bin is barely a quarter full. Use a 10-yard dumpster for heavy debris and fill it only part way.

Will a 40-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

It fits only a long, wide driveway. The bin is about 22 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 7 to 8 feet tall. Most homes use a jobsite or a permitted street spot instead. Measure first and plan parking.

How long can I keep a 40-yard dumpster?

Most rentals last 7 to 14 days, with a 7-day term the most common. An extra day typically costs $10 to $25. Choose your rental length at booking and extend early if the job runs long.
