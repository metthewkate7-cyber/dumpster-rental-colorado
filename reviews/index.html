Quick answer

There are no customer reviews on this page yet. That is honest, not a gap we hide. Reviews are collected from real customers after their rental is complete. Once the first reviews arrive, they appear here with the city and the size rented. No review on this site is ever written by us.

How customers leave a review

After your dumpster is picked up, we ask you for a review. You can leave it in a short message, by phone, or through the contact form. Tell us your city, the size you rented, and how the job went. That is all it takes.

You can also leave a review on our Google Business Profile once it is live. Reviews left there and reviews sent to us directly are both real customer words. We publish them here as they arrive.

What our review process is

Every review on this page comes from a real rental. We check that the reviewer booked with us and that the rental was completed. We do not pay for reviews. We do not ask friends or staff to write them. We do not edit the words, except to remove personal details like a full street address or a phone number.

Good reviews and bad reviews both stay up. A bad review tells us what to fix, and it tells future customers what to expect. If a review points to a real problem, we answer it and fix the problem first.

What we ask reviewers to cover

Four details make a review useful. First, your city. Delivery in Denver is not the same as delivery in Durango. Second, your size. A 10-yard cleanout is a different job from a 40-yard demolition. Third, delivery on time and in the right spot. Fourth, driveway care and pickup timing.

Those four details help the next customer more than a star count alone. When reviews arrive, each one will show the city, the size, and those four points in the reviewer's own words.

Why this page is empty today

This site is new. The first rentals have not finished yet, so no customer has had a chance to review us. We could have filled this page with made-up praise. We chose not to. A blank page with an honest note is worth more than five fake stars.

Check back after the first rentals complete. The first real reviews will appear here as soon as they arrive. Until then, the about page explains what we do and what we do not claim, and the FAQ page answers the questions most customers ask before they book.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a star rating?

Not yet. A star rating needs real reviews behind it. Until customers leave reviews, we show no rating anywhere on this site. No rating is shown in our search results either.

Can I trust a site with no reviews?

That is a fair question. Read the about page, the prices page, and the booking terms. Prices on this site are published market ranges with sources. Your exact price is confirmed by phone before you pay anything. No payment is taken on the booking page.

How will I know when reviews appear?

They will show on this page with the city and size for each rental. We do not send a notice. Check this page after your own rental if you want to see your review posted.
