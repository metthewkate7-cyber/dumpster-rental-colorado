Highlands Ranch local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Highlands Ranch is an unincorporated community in Douglas County, not a city. It is a census-designated place with 103,444 residents in the 2020 Census. The ZIP codes are 80126, 80129, and 80130, with 80163 for post office boxes. The community sits south of Littleton and west of Lone Tree. Main areas are Northridge, Westridge, Eastridge, and Southridge.

Highlands Ranch is the largest unincorporated community in Colorado. The Highlands Ranch Metropolitan District runs local services. The Highlands Ranch Community Association is one of the largest homeowner groups in the country. The Highlands Ranch Mansion and Daniels Park draw visitors year round. Miles of trails link the villages.

Permits follow the county rule in Highlands Ranch. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. Douglas County does not allow dumpsters on a public road. Keep the box on private property or in the driveway. The Highlands Ranch Community Association may ask for advance notice, so check your HOA rules before you book.

Most Highlands Ranch loads go to the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site in Aurora. The site sits at 3500 S Gun Club Road. It is the main metro landfill for Douglas County loads. Paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and electronics never go in a dumpster. Take those to the Douglas County household hazardous waste program instead.
