Quick answer

Same-day dumpster rental means the bin arrives on the day you book, when a truck and a bin are free in your area. Call before midday for the best chance. Next-day delivery is the safer bet when same-day slots are full. Published Colorado prices run from about $300 for a 10-yard bin to about $906 for a 40-yard bin on national average.

How same day dumpster rental works

Same-day delivery depends on two things: a free truck and a free bin in your area. Trucks run set routes each day. A slot opens when a job finishes early or a booking cancels.

Call before midday for the best chance. Morning calls catch the truck before the route is set. Afternoon calls can still work, but the odds drop fast.

Online booking shows the next open slot. If today is full, tomorrow is the next option. Book the slot and the truck comes on that day.

Best jobs for same-day delivery

  • Storm cleanup that cannot wait
  • Emergency cleanout before a sale or move
  • Roofing crew that needs a bin today
  • Construction job that filled the bin early
  • HOA deadline that needs waste gone now
  • Property turnover with tenants moving in

For planned jobs, book a day ahead. Next-day delivery is nearly always open, and it costs the same as a standard booking.

How to get a bin today

  1. Call before midday, or book online and pick today.
  2. Tell us the size, the address, and where the bin goes.
  3. Clear the drop spot before the truck arrives.
  4. Put plywood boards down if you want driveway protection.
  5. Load the bin as soon as it lands.

The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. Clear cars, bins, and low branches from the path before delivery.

When same-day is not possible

Same-day slots fill up. When they do, next-day delivery is the next option. Book the next open slot online and the truck comes on that day.

Street placement needs a permit in most cities, and permits take days. A same-day bin must go on your own driveway or lot. Denver street permits take about 5 business days through DOTI.

Remote towns have fewer trucks. Same-day delivery is most likely in Denver, Colorado Springs, Aurora, and Fort Collins. Smaller towns may need a day of lead time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really get a dumpster the same day in Colorado?

Yes, when a truck and a bin are free in your area. Call before midday for the best chance. Morning calls catch the truck before the route is set. If today is full, next-day delivery is the next option.

What time do I need to call for same-day delivery?

Call before midday. Trucks set their routes in the morning, and slots fill fast. Afternoon calls can still work, but the odds drop. Online booking shows the next open slot in real time.

Does same-day dumpster rental cost extra?

Not always. It depends on the hauler and the route. Some haulers charge the standard rate for a same-day slot. Ask at booking whether a same-day slot carries a fee before you confirm.

What size dumpster can I get same-day?

Small and mid-size bins have the best odds. The 10-yard, 15-yard, and 20-yard bins are the most likely to be free. Large 30-yard and 40-yard bins are fewer in number, so book early in the day for those.

Can I get a same-day dumpster on the street?

No, not usually. Street placement needs a city permit, and permits take days. A same-day bin must go on your own driveway or lot. Denver street permits take about 5 business days through DOTI.
