20 Yard Dumpster Rental in Colorado

A 20 yard dumpster rental is the most rented size in Colorado. It handles kitchen remodels, multi-room cleanouts, flooring jobs, and roofing tear-offs in one trip.

Quick answer

A 20-yard dumpster holds about 6 pickup loads, or 110 to 130 trash bags. It measures about 22 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 4 to 4.5 feet high. It is the standard pick for a kitchen remodel or a multi-room cleanout. The national average price was $679 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com.

What a 20 yard dumpster rental holds

A 20-yard bin holds about 110 to 130 trash bags. That is 6 full pickup truck loads. It is the size most Colorado renters end up with, because it covers the jobs people actually do.

A full kitchen remodel fits: cabinets, counters, flooring, drywall, and old appliances. A multi-room cleanout fits too. So does a roofing tear-off on a mid-size home. The bin is long and low, so loading stays easy.

The 20-yard size is the sweet spot between cost and capacity. The 10-yard bin is cheaper but fills too fast on a real remodel. The 30-yard bin holds more but costs more and needs more space.

At 22 feet long, the bin needs a full driveway or a clear stretch of street. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. Measure your driveway before you book if it is short.

Weight allowance and overage fees

Most 20-yard rentals include 2 to 4 tons of debris. Household junk and light building waste rarely hit the limit. Roofing shingles are the exception. One layer of shingles from a mid-size roof can weigh 2 to 3 tons on its own.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. HBS Trash in Denver charges $75 per ton over the limit. All American Dumpster in Colorado Springs charges $65 per ton. Load heavy material first and low, and keep the pile level with the top rail.

Do not fill a 20-yard bin with concrete or dirt. Heavy material hits the weight limit at a fraction of the volume. A 10-yard dumpster is the right bin for heavy debris.

Best jobs for a 20-yard dumpster

  • Full kitchen remodel, cabinets and counters out
  • Multi-room cleanout, three or more rooms
  • Flooring removal through a whole home
  • Roofing tear-off on a mid-size home
  • Basement or garage cleanout with furniture
  • Small renovation with drywall, wood, and fixtures
  • Estate cleanout of a mid-size home

For a whole-house cleanout, step up to the 30-yard dumpster. For a single room, the 10-yard dumpster costs less.

When a 20-yard dumpster is the wrong size

A 20-yard bin is wrong for a whole-house cleanout with furniture in every room. You will fill it and need a second haul. A 30-yard bin does the same job in one trip for less than two hauls.

It is also wrong for major demolition. Torn-out framing, joists, and roofing from a big demo need a taller bin. The 40-yard dumpster fits light bulky demo waste best.

And it is wrong for a full load of heavy debris. Concrete, dirt, and brick hit the 2 to 4 ton limit fast. Use a small bin for heavy loads, and fill it only part way.

Where a 20-yard dumpster fits

A 20-yard bin needs a full driveway. At 22 feet long, it takes most of a standard two-car drive. Check that your cars can park on the street or in the garage while the bin is in place.

Put plywood boards under the rails. The bin is heavy when full, and boards spread the load across the driveway. They stop scratches on concrete and ruts in asphalt.

Street placement may need a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. In Colorado Springs, street placement needs a Land Use Review revocable permit. Check the Colorado permit guide for your city.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 20-yard dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges for a 20-yard bin run $330 to $815. A typical local price is $400 to $560. The national average was $679 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How many trash bags fit in a 20-yard dumpster?

About 110 to 130 standard trash bags fit in a 20-yard dumpster. That is about 6 pickup truck loads. Break down large items to fit more in, and keep the load level with the top rail.

Is a 20-yard dumpster big enough for a kitchen remodel?

Yes, for most kitchens. Cabinets, counters, flooring, drywall, and old appliances from a full kitchen remodel fit in a 20-yard bin. A very large kitchen with an island and a pantry may need a 30-yard bin.

Can I put shingles in a 20-yard dumpster?

Yes. A 20-yard bin is a common pick for a roofing tear-off on a mid-size home. Watch the weight. Shingles are heavy, and one layer from a mid-size roof can weigh 2 to 3 tons, close to the 2 to 4 ton allowance.

Will a 20-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

It fits most two-car driveways, but it takes most of the length. The bin is about 22 feet long and 8 feet wide. Measure your driveway first, and plan where your cars will park during the rental.

How long can I keep a 20-yard dumpster?

Most bins stay out 7 to 14 days, with 7 days the most common term. Each extra day typically costs $10 to $25. Choose your rental length at booking and extend early if the job runs long.
