What size fits your job in Fort Collins?

Fort Collins jobs split three ways: student move-outs, remodels in Old Town homes, and construction on the Harmony corridor. Student move-outs take a 10-yard dumpster. Remodels take a 20-yard dumpster. Construction jobs take a 30-yard or 40-yard dumpster. Pick the size that fits both the job and the lot.

  • 10-yard dumpster: small cleanout, one room, student move-out, or heavy debris like concrete and dirt. About 3 pickup loads. About 50 to 70 bags.
  • 15-yard dumpster: mid-size cleanout, two-room remodel, or flooring through a house. About 4 to 5 pickup loads. About 80 to 100 bags.
  • 20-yard dumpster: the most rented size in Fort Collins. Kitchen remodel, multi-room cleanout, flooring, or roofing tear-off. About 6 pickup loads. About 110 to 130 bags.
  • 30-yard dumpster: whole-house cleanout, major renovation, or large construction debris. About 9 pickup loads. About 170 to 190 bags. Not for heavy debris.
  • 40-yard dumpster: major demolition or large light bulky debris. About 12 pickup loads. About 230 to 250 bags. Never for concrete, dirt, or brick.

Permits are clear here. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a public street or sidewalk needs a Permit to Obstruct, which costs $25. Fort Collins has two disposal sites. The Central Diversion and Transfer Station sits at 2388 West Trilby Road and takes household and construction debris. Driveways in Fort Collins are mostly concrete in the newer suburbs, with older asphalt in Old Town and Midtown. Concrete holds a loaded dumpster well when boards sit under the rails. How delivery and pickup work in Fort Collins

Use the booking page to book in three steps. First comes your size. Then comes your delivery date and rental days. Last comes your address and phone number. We call to confirm your size, dates, and exact price. The booking page takes no payment.

Delivery day is simple. Clear the driveway spot before the truck arrives. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. It also needs 25 to 30 feet of clear space above for the lift. Move cars, low branches, and bins out of the way. Short driveways in Old Town need a smaller dumpster. Wide driveways on the Harmony corridor fit a big dumpster. The driver sets boards under the rails, sets the dumpster down, and checks the spot with you.

Load it right and you dodge extra fees. Fill the dumpster level with the top edge. Never fill above it. Put heavy material at the bottom. Put light material on top. Keep paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, and hazardous waste out. Larimer County runs hazardous waste drop-off for those items. Never mix concrete with household junk in a big dumpster. Unsure about an item? Check what can go in a dumpster or call us first.

Pickup is one call away. Call or message us when the dumpster is full or your rental days are up. We schedule pickup, usually the next business day. Keep the area around the dumpster clear so the truck can hook it and go. Do not park cars in front of it on pickup day. We haul it to the transfer station and your job is done.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Fort Collins?

You need a Permit to Obstruct when the dumpster sits on a public street or sidewalk in Fort Collins. It costs $25 from the Engineering Department at 281 North College Avenue, phone (970) 221-6605. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit.

How much does a 10-yard dumpster cost in Fort Collins?

Published market prices put a 10-yard dumpster at $399 to $499 for 5 days in Fort Collins. The source is Prime Dumpster, checked November 13, 2026. ZTERS lists about $600 for a 10-yard dumpster over 10 days. Your exact price is confirmed when you book.

What is the covered-load rule in Larimer County?

Larimer County requires loads to be covered when they travel to the landfill or transfer station. The hauler covers the dumpster before transport, so you do not need to tarp it yourself. Keep debris level with the top edge so the cover fits.

What size dumpster fits a CSU student move-out?

A 10-yard dumpster fits most student move-outs near Campus West. It holds about 3 pickup loads and 50 to 70 bags. A 15-yard dumpster fits a shared house cleanout with furniture. Book early at the end of the semester, because trucks fill fast.

Where does Fort Collins dumpster debris go?

Fort Collins debris goes to the Central Diversion and Transfer Station at 2388 West Trilby Road. The Larimer County North Landfill in Wellington opened in August 2026. Hazardous items never go in a dumpster and need a separate drop-off.

What can I not put in a dumpster in Fort Collins?

You cannot load paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, appliances with freon, or hazardous waste in Fort Collins. Larimer County runs household hazardous waste drop-off for those items. Ask us before you load anything odd and we will point you to the right place.
