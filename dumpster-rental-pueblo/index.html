What size fits your job in Pueblo?

Pueblo jobs split three ways: historic home remodels, steel-mill and construction debris, and whole-house cleanouts. Historic remodels take a 20-yard dumpster. Construction debris takes a 30-yard dumpster. Whole-house cleanouts take a 30-yard dumpster. Narrow streets change the math, so measure first.

  • 10-yard dumpster: small cleanout, one room, small bathroom, or heavy debris like concrete and dirt. About 3 pickup loads. About 50 to 70 bags.
  • 15-yard dumpster: mid-size cleanout, two-room remodel, or flooring through a house. About 4 to 5 pickup loads. About 80 to 100 bags.
  • 20-yard dumpster: the most rented size in Pueblo. Kitchen remodel, multi-room cleanout, flooring, or roofing tear-off. About 6 pickup loads. About 110 to 130 bags.

  • 40-yard dumpster: major demolition or large light bulky debris. About 12 pickup loads. About 230 to 250 bags. Never for concrete, dirt, or brick.
