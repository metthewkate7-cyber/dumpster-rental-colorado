Quick answer

An estate cleanout dumpster is a roll-off bin for clearing a full home: furniture, boxes, clothes, and household items from every room. The 30-yard bin is the common pick for a full house. Most rentals run 7 to 14 days, and you can extend if sorting takes longer. Published Colorado prices run from about $350 for a 20-yard bin in Colorado Springs to about $809 for a 30-yard bin on national average.

What estate cleanout dumpster rental covers

Estate rental covers the waste a full home clear-out makes. Furniture, mattresses, boxes, clothes, kitchenware, old appliances, and general household junk all fit. The bin sits in the driveway while the family works through the house room by room.

There is no rush. Most rentals run 7 to 14 days, and you can extend for a daily fee. Sorting a lifetime of belongings takes time, and the bin waits while you work.

The rental includes delivery, pickup, disposal, and a set weight allowance. You load it yourself, on your own schedule. No crew, no hourly rate.

Best jobs for an estate cleanout dumpster

  • Clearing a loved one's home after a loss
  • Clearing a house for sale or probate
  • Downsizing a parent into a smaller home
  • Clearing a long-held family home
  • Hoarding cleanout with years of stored items
  • Clearing a rental property between tenants

For a full house, a 30-yard dumpster is the common pick. For a small home or a partial clear-out, use a 20-yard dumpster.

When in doubt, size up. A second haul costs more than the step up to the next size. Furniture is bulky, and it fills a bin fast.

How to sort an estate with a dumpster

Work room by room. Keep one room clear as a sorting space. Label boxes as keep, donate, and toss. The toss pile goes straight into the bin.

Donate first, toss second. Charities take furniture, clothes, and working appliances. What is left goes in the bin. That order keeps the bin from filling with items someone else could use.

Watch for hazardous items. Old paint, chemicals, and batteries cannot go in the bin. Set them aside for a household hazardous waste drop-off. In Greeley, that is the North Household Hazardous Waste Facility at 1311 N 17th Ave.

Rules for estate cleanout rentals

Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled, and the driver may refuse pickup. Break down furniture to fit more in.

Do not load hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, or asbestos. Do not load refrigerators with freon unless the freon is removed first. Check the house for old paint cans and chemicals before you start.

Ask for plywood boards under the rails at booking. Boards protect the driveway from scratches and ruts, which matters when the house is about to sell.

When Estate Cleanout Dumpster Rental Is Wrong for You

An estate bin is wrong for construction debris. Wood, drywall, and tile need a jobsite bin. A construction dumpster fits that job better. You pay for a whole house plan you do not need.

It is wrong for heavy concrete too. Concrete hits the weight limit fast. A concrete dumpster in a 10-yard dumpster is the right call.

Skip it for a single room. One room does not need a whole house plan. A 10-yard dumpster or a residential dumpster fits that job better.

Frequently asked questions

What size dumpster do I need for an estate cleanout?

A small home or partial clear-out fits a 20-yard bin. A mid-size home needs a 30-yard bin. A large home or a hoarding cleanout needs a 40-yard bin. Furniture is bulky, so size up when in doubt.

How much does an estate cleanout dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges run $330 to $500 for a 20-yard bin in Denver and $345 to $550 for a 30-yard bin. The national average for a 30-yard bin was $809 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How long can I keep an estate cleanout dumpster?

You can keep the bin 7 to 14 days, and 7 days is the most common term. Extra days typically cost $10 to $25 per day. Sorting an estate takes time, so extend early if you need more days.

What can I not put in an estate cleanout dumpster?

No hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, or asbestos. No refrigerators with freon unless the freon is removed first. Old paint cans and chemicals should go to a household hazardous waste drop-off.

Should I donate before I toss?

Yes. Donate furniture, clothes, and working appliances first. Charities take items in good shape. What is left goes in the bin. That order keeps the bin from filling with items someone else could use.
