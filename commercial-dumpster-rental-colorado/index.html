Quick answer

A commercial dumpster is a roll-off bin for business waste: office cleanouts, retail remodels, warehouse clearouts, and jobsite debris. Sizes run 10 to 40 yards, and repeat jobs can use scheduled swaps. Published Colorado prices run from about $330 for a 20-yard bin in Denver to about $906 for a 40-yard bin on national average.

What commercial dumpster rental colorado covers

Commercial rental covers business waste of every kind. Office cleanouts, retail remodels, restaurant refits, warehouse clearouts, and construction debris all fit. The bin goes on your lot, loading dock, or jobsite.

One-off jobs get a single bin for 7 to 14 days. Ongoing jobs get scheduled swaps: a full bin out, an empty bin in, on a set day each week. Tell us the pace of the job and we will set the swap schedule.

Multiple bins can run at one site. A 20-yard bin for light waste and a 10-yard bin for heavy debris is a common split. It keeps weight limits in check and avoids overage fees.

Scheduled swaps and repeat service

Jobs that run for weeks need a swap plan, not a single bin. We pull the full bin and drop an empty one on the same visit. The crew keeps working and the site stays clear.

Set the swap day when you book. Weekly swaps fit most remodels. Large demo jobs may need two swaps a week. Tell us the waste pace and we will match it.

Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled, and it delays the swap. Load heavy material low and light waste on top.

Permits and site rules for businesses

A bin on your own lot needs no permit. A bin on a public street or sidewalk needs a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. In Fort Collins, a Permit to Obstruct costs $25.

Check loading dock access before delivery day. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance, plus 25 to 30 feet of vertical clearance. Low canopies and tight turns are the usual problem.

Keep the area around the bin clear for the truck. A blocked bin means a missed pickup and a reschedule fee risk. Mark the drop spot with cones if the lot is busy.

Frequently asked questions

What size dumpster does a business need?

A small office cleanout fits a 10-yard bin. An office floor or small retail remodel fits a 20-yard bin. A full refit fits a 30-yard bin. Large construction or demolition needs a 40-yard bin. Tell us the job and we will confirm the size.

Can I get scheduled dumpster swaps for a long job?

Yes. We pull the full bin and drop an empty one on the same visit, on a set day each week. Large jobs can run two swaps a week. Set the swap schedule when you book.

How much does a commercial dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges run $330 to $500 for a 20-yard bin in Denver and $345 to $550 for a 30-yard bin. The national average for a 40-yard bin was $906 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster at my business?

No permit is needed when the bin sits on your own lot. Street or sidewalk placement usually needs a city permit. Denver requires a DOTI street permit with about 5 business days of lead time.

Can I rent more than one dumpster at a time?

Yes. Multiple bins can run at one site. A common split is a 20-yard bin for light waste and a 10-yard bin for heavy debris. It keeps weight limits in check and avoids overage fees.
