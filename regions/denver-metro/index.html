Dumpster Rental in the Denver Metro Area, Colorado

The Denver metro area is the largest dumpster rental market in Colorado. It covers Denver plus Aurora, Lakewood, Thornton, Arvada, Westminster, and the suburbs around them, with more than 2 million people in the region.

What the Denver metro area is

The Denver metro area is the urban core of Colorado. Denver sits at the center. Aurora is the second city to the east. Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, and Thornton ring the core. Smaller cities like Golden, Louisville, and Superior sit on the west edge near the foothills.

Housing here is old and new at once. Downtown, Capitol Hill, Highlands, and Park Hill have homes over a century old. Stapleton, now called Central Park, and the south suburbs have new builds. Old homes need remodel boxes. New builds need construction boxes. Both keep the market busy year round.

Traffic and access shape every job. Downtown alleys are tight. Suburban cul-de-sacs are short. The truck needs 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. Measure before you book.

Prices in the Denver metro area

Denver metro prices run higher than most of Colorado. Published market ranges put a 20 yard box near $330 to $500 and a 30 yard box near $345 to $550, with 14 days and 3 tons included in some quotes. A 30 yard box from one local operator runs $535 to $635 for 14 days and 3 tons.

Overage rates in the metro run $70 to $75 per ton with some operators, and up to $85 per ton in other quotes. Extra days run about $5 per day in some Denver quotes. Delivery fees can sit apart from the base price, so ask whether delivery is bundled.

Permits and disposal in the metro

Denver sets the strictest rule in the metro. A street box needs a Right-of-Way Revocable Street Occupancy Permit from Denver DOTI at 303-446-3759. It takes about 5 business days. The fee typically runs $75 to $150. Only the hauling company can pull this permit. Plan around that lead time.

The suburbs use the universal rule. Driveway placement needs no permit in most cases. Street placement needs a permit from that city's Public Works office. Check with Aurora, Lakewood, Thornton, or your own city before a street placement. Fees and lead times differ by city.

Most metro loads go to Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site. It is the main metro landfill. Aurora yard waste can also go through city organics programs for small loads. Our permit guide has the full city table.

Which size fits metro jobs

The 20 yard box leads the metro. It fits kitchen remodels, multi room cleanouts, and flooring jobs in the older homes of Denver and Aurora. The 10 yard box fits garage cleanouts and small bathroom remodels. It also fits heavy debris like concrete and shingles.

Whole house cleanouts in the suburbs need a 30 yard box. Large construction and demolition need a 30 or 40 yard box. Both are for light, bulky debris only. Tight drives in older Denver areas may only fit a 10 or 15 yard box. Measure first.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Denver?

Only for street placement. A box fully on your driveway needs no permit. A street box needs a Denver DOTI permit, and only the hauling company can pull it.

What does a 20 yard dumpster cost in Denver?

Published market ranges put a 20 yard box near $330 to $500 in Denver, often with 14 days and 3 tons included. Your exact price is confirmed at booking.

Which metro cities allow same day delivery?

Same day delivery depends on the route and the city, not on a fixed list. Call before mid morning for the best shot. Aurora and Arvada operators advertise same day service most often.

Can a big dumpster fit in a downtown Denver alley?

Often not. Alleys are tight and low wires are common. A 10 or 15 yard box fits where a 30 yard box cannot. Tell the hauler about the alley before delivery day.

Where does metro dumpster debris go?

Most loads go to Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site. Recyclables and electronics go to county sites like the Boulder County Recycling Center and Eco-Cycle CHaRM for nearby cities.
