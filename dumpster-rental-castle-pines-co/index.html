Castle Pines local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Castle Pines is a home rule city in Douglas County. It had 11,036 residents in the 2020 Census. The main ZIP code is 80108. The city sits on I-25 between Lone Tree and Castle Rock. Main areas are The Village at Castle Pines, The Canyons, and Legacy Village.

Castle Pines became a city in 2007, so it is one of the youngest cities in Colorado. The Village at Castle Pines is a gated golf course community. The Canyons is the newer master planned area on the east side. Pine trees and open space shape most streets. Most homes are newer, with wide driveways that fit a 30-yard dumpster.

Permits follow the city rule in Castle Pines. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A street placement needs a City of Castle Pines right-of-way permit first. Check with Castle Pines Public Works before you book a street placement, and allow extra days for approval. Permit fees and lead times change, so confirm the current rules with the city.

Most Castle Pines loads go to the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site in Aurora. The site sits at 3500 S Gun Club Road. It is the main metro landfill for Douglas County loads. Paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and electronics never go in a dumpster. Take those to the Douglas County household hazardous waste program instead.
