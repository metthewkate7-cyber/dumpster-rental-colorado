How Long Can I Keep a Dumpster in Colorado?

How long can I keep a dumpster?

Most rentals run 7 to 14 days. Seven days is the most common term. Some haulers offer 10 or 14 days in the base price. Short jobs may use a 3 day term. The term is set in your quote, so read it before you book.

The clock starts on delivery day. Delivery day is day one. A box delivered on a Monday and picked up the next Monday has run 8 days. Many renters miss this and pay for one extra day. Count from delivery, not from the order date.

Extension costs: what an extra day runs

Extensions cost $5 to $45 per day across markets. The most common range is $10 to $25 per day. Colorado operators publish $5 per day in some Denver quotes and $15 to $25 per day in Colorado Springs quotes. Your rate depends on your hauler and your city.

Do the math before you extend. Four extra days at $15 is $60. A second week at $25 per day is $175. At some point, a fresh rental or a swap costs less than a long extension. Ask the hauler to compare the two numbers.

Extension depends on availability. If the box is booked for another job, it may not extend. Call early. Call at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup. Once a driver is dispatched, rerouting may be impossible.

How to extend without a surprise fee

Call before the pickup date, not after. Tell the hauler how many extra days you need. Ask the daily rate and get it in writing. Ask whether the weight limit changes. It does not. The tonnage stays the same even when the days grow.

Keep loading while you wait. A box that sits half full wastes the extension. Fill it level and under the fill line. Our overfill guide shows the fill line rule.

If you finish early, call for pickup. Most pickups happen within 1 to 3 business days of your call. You do not need to be home for pickup. Keep the area clear so the driver can reach the box.

Swap-outs: when a fresh box beats an extension

A swap is a full box hauled away and a fresh box dropped in its place. It fits big jobs that fill a box before the work ends. A renovation that fills a 20 yard box in 4 days needs a swap, not an extension.

Ask the hauler the swap price when you book. It is often close to a new rental. Compare that with the extension cost. A swap keeps the job moving. An extension on a full box does not.

Plan the swap call early. A swap needs a truck slot like a new delivery. Same day swaps are rare. Book the swap a day or two before the box fills.

What happens if you keep it too long

The hauler charges the daily rate for each extra day. Some haulers auto charge once the window passes. Others dispatch a driver and add a return trip fee. Either way, the cost grows each day.

A box that sits too long can also draw a city or HOA complaint. Some HOAs limit a box to a set number of days. Some cities limit street boxes. Check both rules before you extend. Our permit guide covers HOA and street rules.

The fix is simple. Pick a realistic rental length at booking. Add a buffer day if your job is unsure. Call early if you need more time. A short call beats a surprise bill.

Rental length and your permit

Your rental length and your permit length are two different clocks. A street permit may run fewer days than your rental. A permit for 7 days does not cover a 14 day rental. Check the permit dates when you extend.

HOA rules add a third clock. Some HOAs limit a box to a set number of days on the drive. An extension past the HOA limit can draw a fine. Check the HOA limit before you extend the rental.

Line the three clocks up at booking. Pick a rental length, a permit length, and an HOA window that all fit the job. A short call to the city and the HOA before booking saves a fine later.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are included in a dumpster rental?

Most rentals include 7 days. Some include 10 or 14 days. The exact number is in your quote. Ask for it in writing before you book.

When does the rental clock start?

On delivery day. Delivery day is day one, not the day you order. A box delivered Monday and picked up the next Monday has used 8 days.

Can I extend my rental by one day?

Yes, in most cases. Call at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup. The fee is often $10 to $25 per day. If the box is booked for another job, it may not extend.

What happens if I keep the dumpster too long?

The hauler charges the daily rate for each extra day. Some haulers dispatch a driver to pick it up. An unplanned pickup can add a trip fee on top of the daily rate.

Can I get a pickup earlier than planned?

Yes. Call the hauler and ask for an early pickup. Most pickups happen within 1 to 3 business days of your call. Early pickup does not usually earn a refund.
