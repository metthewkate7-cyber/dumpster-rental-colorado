Columbine local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Columbine is an unincorporated community in Jefferson County, not a city. It is a census-designated place with 25,229 residents in the 2020 Census. Most of the community sits in Jefferson County, and a small slice runs into Arapahoe County. The ZIP codes are 80123 and 80128. Most addresses here carry a Littleton mailing address, but the homes sit outside the Littleton city limits. Main areas are Clement Park, Columbine Hills, and the Bowles Avenue corridor.

Clement Park anchors the community. The park covers 193 acres at 7306 West Bowles Avenue. It holds the Columbine Memorial, ball fields, playgrounds, and a skate park. The Foothills Park and Recreation District runs the park. Columbine High School stands at 6201 South Pierce Street. The Columbine Library sits at 7706 West Bowles Avenue. Jefferson County Public Schools, known as Jeffco, serves the area. Homes are mostly post-war and newer subdivisions on quiet cul-de-sacs.

Permits follow the county rule in Columbine. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a road or in a public right of way needs a Jefferson County right-of-way permit first. The permit costs $50, plus 2.5 percent on a card payment. The county asks for 48 hours to review the online application. Many subdivisions have HOAs, so check your HOA rules on dumpster placement and time limits.

Debris from Columbine loads goes to the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site, known as DADS. The site sits at 3500 South Gun Club Road in Aurora. It is the main metro landfill for the south side. Paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and electronics never go in a dumpster. Colorado bans electronics from landfills. Take those to a county hazardous waste site instead. Cul-de-sacs are common here, so flag the access when you book.
