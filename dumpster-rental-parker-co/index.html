Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Parker?

No permit is usually needed when the dumpster sits on your own driveway. Street or sidewalk placement needs a town permit from the Town of Parker. Check with the town before booking a street spot, and allow extra days for approval.

Does Parker run trash collection?
