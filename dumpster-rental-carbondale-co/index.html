Carbondale local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Carbondale is a town in Garfield County, with 6,434 residents at the 2020 Census. The ZIP code is 81623. Main areas are downtown Main Street, the north side, and River Valley Ranch. Homes mix historic houses, condos, and newer subdivisions. Carbondale is a bedroom town for Aspen workers and a hub for valley builders.

Permits follow the universal rule in Carbondale. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street or in a public right of way needs town approval first. Check with the Town of Carbondale Public Works department before you place a street dumpster. Downtown Main Street is busy, so street placement needs care.

Debris from Carbondale loads goes to the South Canyon Landfill at 1250 County Road 134 in Glenwood Springs, or to the Pitkin County Solid Waste Center near Aspen. Both take household waste and construction debris. Paint, oil, and batteries never go in a dumpster. The town runs an active recycling program, so separate clean cardboard and metal where you can.
