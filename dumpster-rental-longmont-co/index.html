Longmont at a glance

Longmont has 98,885 people at the 2020 census, mostly in Boulder County with its east side in Weld County. The main ZIP codes are 80501, 80503, and 80504. Neighborhoods include downtown Longmont, Prospect New Town, Southmoor, Clover Basin, Garden Acres, Longmont Estates, and West Longmont. Longmont housing runs from older homes in the historic core to pricier homes in Prospect and West Longmont, with newer subdivisions on the edges. The median home value sits near $541,400.
