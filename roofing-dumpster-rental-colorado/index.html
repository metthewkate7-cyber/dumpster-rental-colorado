Quick answer

A roofing dumpster is a roll-off bin for tear-off waste: shingles, felt, flashing, and nails. Shingles are heavy, so roofers usually pick a 10-yard or 20-yard bin. Published Colorado prices run from about $300 for a 10-yard bin in Colorado Springs to about $679 for a 20-yard bin on national average.

What roofing dumpster rental colorado covers

Roofing rental covers the waste a tear-off makes. Asphalt shingles, wood shakes, felt underlayment, flashing, drip edge, and nails all fit. The bin sits in the driveway or on the street near the house.

Shingles are the heavy part. One layer from a mid-size roof can weigh 2 to 3 tons. Two layers weigh twice that. The bin size must match the weight, not just the volume.

The rental includes delivery, pickup, disposal, and a set weight allowance. Most rentals run 7 to 14 days, which covers a standard re-roof with time to spare.

Best jobs for a roofing dumpster

  • Asphalt shingle tear-off, one or two layers
  • Wood shake removal
  • Full re-roof with underlayment out
  • Metal roof removal
  • Flat roof tear-off, membrane and insulation
  • Gutter and fascia replacement with the roof
  • Storm damage roof replacement

For a mid-size roof, a 20-yard dumpster is the common pick. For a small roof or a single layer, a 10-yard dumpster works.

Weight: the roofing trap

Shingles fool people. They look light in a pile, but a full bin of shingles can weigh 4 tons or more. The weight allowance on a 20-yard bin is usually 2 to 4 tons. A full bin of shingles can blow past it.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. HBS Trash in Denver charges $75 per ton over the limit. All American Dumpster in Colorado Springs charges $65 per ton. A second small bin is often cheaper than the overage on one big bin.

Keep nails and metal flashing in the bin too. A magnet sweep of the yard after the job is standard practice, but nails still end up in the load. That is fine. Just keep the load level with the top rail.

Where to place a roofing dumpster

Place the bin as close to the house as the truck can reach. A short carry from the roof edge saves the crew hours. The driveway is the usual spot.

Put plywood boards under the rails. Shingle loads are heavy, and boards spread the weight across the driveway. They stop cracks in concrete and ruts in asphalt.

Street placement may need a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. Check the Colorado permit guide for your city.

When Roofing Dumpster Rental Is Wrong for You

A roofing bin is wrong for mixed remodel debris. Drywall, wood, and tile need a bigger mix. A construction dumpster fits that job better.

It is wrong for concrete or dirt too. Heavy material hits the weight limit fast. A concrete dumpster in a 10-yard dumpster is the right call.

Skip it for a tiny repair. A few shingles do not need a full bin. A 10-yard dumpster handles a small roof job for less. A small bin also costs less than a big one.

Frequently asked questions

What size dumpster do I need for a roofing tear-off?

A small roof with one layer fits a 10-yard bin. A mid-size roof with one layer fits a 20-yard bin. A mid-size roof with two layers may need a 20-yard bin plus a second haul, or a 30-yard bin. Count the layers before you book.

How much does a roofing dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges run from about $300 for a 10-yard bin in Colorado Springs to $330 to $500 for a 20-yard bin in Denver. The national average for a 20-yard bin was $679 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How heavy are roofing shingles in a dumpster?

One layer of shingles from a mid-size roof weighs about 2 to 3 tons. Two layers weigh about twice that. A 20-yard bin usually includes 2 to 4 tons, so a full bin of shingles can hit the limit fast.

Can I put wood shakes in a roofing dumpster?

Yes. Wood shakes, felt, flashing, and nails all go in a roofing dumpster. Wood shakes are lighter than shingles, so the weight limit is less of a worry. Keep the load level with the top rail.

Do I need a permit for a roofing dumpster?

No permit is needed when the bin sits on your own driveway. Street placement usually needs a city permit. Denver requires a DOTI street permit with about 5 business days of lead time.
