Windsor at a glance

Windsor has 32,716 people at the 2020 census, split between Weld and Larimer counties, with the larger share in Weld. The main ZIP codes are 80550, 80551, and 80528. Neighborhoods include Water Valley, Pelican Lakes, Ptarmigan, Eastman Park, River West, Heritage Park, Founders Village, and Harmony Club. The town more than doubled since 2010, and new subdivisions keep rising near the Highway 392 and I-25 interchange. Windsor Lake and Boardwalk Park anchor downtown.

Windsor has about 14,000 housing units, and the median home value sits near $500,000. Water Valley and Pelican Lakes hold resort-style homes around lakes and golf courses, with HOAs and wide driveways. Downtown holds older homes on smaller lots. The town runs no trash or recycling collection of its own, so households hire private haulers like Bunting Disposal, Gallegos Sanitation, Ram Waste Systems, or Waste Management.

A dumpster on your own driveway usually needs no permit in Windsor. A dumpster on the street, sidewalk, or other public right of way needs a town permit. Check with Windsor Public Works before you book a street placement, and allow extra days for approval. Permit fees and lead times change, so confirm the current rules with the town. We do not list a fee here because the town does not publish a fixed one for this permit.

Windsor loads go to the Larimer County Central Transfer Station in Fort Collins for Larimer-side addresses, or to Weld County landfills for Weld-side addresses. Weld County offers a free large item disposal voucher for one trip to the North Weld Landfill in Ault, the Buffalo Ridge Landfill in Keenesburg, or the Front Range Landfill in Erie. Do not put paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, or electronics in the dumpster. Colorado bans electronics from landfills.
