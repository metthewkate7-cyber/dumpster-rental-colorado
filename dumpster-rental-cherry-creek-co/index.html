Cherry Creek local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Cherry Creek is a neighborhood in the City and County of Denver, not a city of its own. It sits about 3 miles southeast of downtown Denver. The ZIP codes are 80206 and 80209. The area runs from 6th Avenue in the north to 1st Avenue in the south. It runs from University Boulevard in the west to Steele Street in the east. Main areas are Cherry Creek North, Cherry Creek East, Country Club, and Belcaro.

Cherry Creek North holds a 16-block shopping district with shops, galleries, and restaurants. Across 1st Avenue sits the Cherry Creek Shopping Center at 3000 East 1st Avenue, with more than 160 stores. The Cherry Creek Trail runs along the creek, and the Cherry Creek Arts Festival fills the streets each summer. Homes mix luxury condos, new duplexes, and high-end single-family houses on tight urban lots.

Permits follow the Denver rule in Cherry Creek. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street, alley, or sidewalk needs a Denver DOTI Revocable Street Occupancy Permit. Only the container company can apply for it, and the company must hold a Denver ROW Dumpster License number. Allow about 5 business days for the permit. The fee typically runs $75 to $150 for a residential street. A container can stay in the right of way for no more than 180 days in a 12-month period. Call Denver DOTI Permit Operations at 303-446-3759 with questions.

Debris from Cherry Creek loads goes to the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site, known as DADS. The site sits at 3500 South Gun Club Road in Aurora. The City and County of Denver owns the site, and Waste Management runs it. Paint, chemicals, and batteries never go in a dumpster. Many condos have boards with rules on dumpster placement and time limits, so check your board rules first. Tight lots and alleys are common here, so flag the access when you book.
