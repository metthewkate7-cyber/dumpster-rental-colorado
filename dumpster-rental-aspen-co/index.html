Aspen local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Aspen is the county seat of Pitkin County, with 7,004 residents at the 2020 Census. The ZIP code is 81611, with 81612 for PO boxes. Main areas are downtown Aspen, the West End, Red Mountain, Aspen Highlands, and Buttermilk. Most homes are condos, townhomes, and high-end single-family houses. Many sit empty part of the year as second homes.

Permits follow the universal rule in Aspen. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street or in a public right of way needs city approval first. Check with the City of Aspen Public Works department before you place a street dumpster. Aspen streets are narrow, and the city guards parking and pedestrian space closely.

Debris from Aspen loads goes to the Pitkin County Solid Waste Center on Service Center Road. The center takes household waste, construction debris, and recycling. It also runs the county household hazardous waste program. Paint, solvents, and batteries never go in a dumpster. Aspen has strict bear rules, so never leave a loaded dumpster open overnight with food waste inside.
