10 Yard Dumpster Rental in Colorado

A 10 yard dumpster rental is the smallest roll-off size we offer in Colorado. It holds about three pickup truck loads and fits in most driveways with room to spare.

Quick answer

A 10-yard dumpster holds about 3 pickup loads, or 50 to 70 trash bags. It measures about 12 to 14 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 3.5 to 4 feet high. It suits small cleanouts, single-room remodels, and heavy debris like concrete. The national average price was $586 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com.

What a 10 yard dumpster rental fits

A 10-yard bin is small, but it holds more than most people expect. It takes about 50 to 70 standard trash bags. That equals three full pickup truck loads.

Think of it as a large skip for one job, not a whole house. It works well when the waste comes from one room or one small project. The low walls make loading easy. You can toss bags over the side without a ladder.

The bin is also the best pick for heavy material. Concrete, dirt, brick, and tile weigh a lot. A small bin keeps the total weight inside the limit. That saves you from overage fees.

Weight allowance and overage fees

Most 10-yard rentals include 1 to 3 tons of debris. The exact allowance depends on the hauler and the job. Heavy debris fills the weight limit fast, even when the bin looks half empty.

Concrete is the classic trap. One cubic yard of broken concrete weighs about 2 tons. A 10-yard bin full of concrete would weigh far more than any truck can haul. So fill heavy bins only part way. A good rule is to fill concrete to about one third of the bin height.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. In Colorado Springs, published overage rates include $65 per ton at All American Dumpster. Dumpster Rental Dogs charges $85 per ton there. Keep the load level and dry to avoid extra charges. Wet debris weighs more.

Best jobs for a 10-yard dumpster

  • Garage, attic, or basement cleanout of one space
  • Small bathroom remodel, tub and tile out
  • Single-room flooring removal, carpet or laminate
  • One-layer roofing tear-off on a small roof
  • Yard cleanup with soil, rock, or sod
  • Concrete, brick, or dirt removal in small amounts
  • Small deck or fence removal

If your job covers two rooms or more, look at the 15-yard dumpster. It costs a little more and holds almost twice the bags.

When a 10-yard dumpster is the wrong size

A 10-yard bin is wrong for a whole-house cleanout. You will fill it on day one and pay for a second haul. A 20-yard dumpster or 30-yard dumpster costs less than two small hauls.

It is also wrong for bulky light waste. Mattresses, furniture, and big boxes fill the space fast. The weight stays low, but the volume runs out. A taller bin holds bulky waste better.

Skip this size for a full kitchen remodel too. Cabinets, counters, and flooring from a full kitchen need more room. The 20-yard size is the usual pick for that job.

Where a 10-yard dumpster fits

A 10-yard bin fits a standard single driveway with ease. It is about as long as two cars parked nose to tail. Most homes in Colorado Springs, Denver, and Fort Collins have room for it.

Put plywood boards under the rails to protect the driveway. The truck driver can place boards for you if you ask at booking. The boards spread the weight and stop scratches on concrete and asphalt.

Street placement may need a city permit. In Denver, a street permit comes from Denver DOTI and takes about 5 business days. In Fort Collins, a Permit to Obstruct costs $25. Check the Colorado permit guide before you book a street spot.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 10-yard dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges for a 10-yard bin run $250 to $660. A typical local price is $300 to $500. The national average was $586 in October 2026, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How many trash bags fit in a 10-yard dumpster?

About 50 to 70 standard trash bags fit in a 10-yard dumpster. That is about 3 pickup truck loads. Bulky items like furniture take up space fast, so the bag count drops when you load large pieces.

Can I put concrete in a 10-yard dumpster?

Yes, a 10-yard bin is the best size for concrete, dirt, brick, and tile. Fill heavy material only part way, to about one third of the bin height, so the total weight stays inside the 1 to 3 ton allowance.

Will a 10-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

Yes, in most cases. The bin is about 12 to 14 feet long and 8 feet wide, about the size of two cars parked nose to tail. The delivery truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance.

How long can I keep a 10-yard dumpster?

Most rentals run 7 to 14 days, with 7 days the most common term. Extra days typically cost $10 to $25 per day. Pick your rental length when you book, and extend early if the job runs long.

What can I not put in a 10-yard dumpster?

No hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, asbestos, or liquids. No refrigerators with freon unless the freon is removed first. Check the full list in our guide to what can go in a dumpster before you load.
