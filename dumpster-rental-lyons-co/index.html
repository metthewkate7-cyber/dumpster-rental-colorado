Lyons local facts: areas, ZIPs, permits, and disposal

Lyons is a town in Boulder County, with 2,209 residents at the 2020 Census. The ZIP code is 80540. Main areas are downtown Main Street, the St. Vrain riverfront, and the foothills roads above town. Homes mix historic sandstone houses, rebuilt flood homes, and newer mountain homes. The 2013 flood reshaped much of the town.

Permits follow the universal rule in Lyons. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a street or in a public right of way needs town approval first. Check with the Town of Lyons Public Works department before you place a street dumpster. Downtown streets are narrow, so street placement needs care.

Debris from Lyons loads goes to the Front Range Landfill at 1830 West County Road 5 in Erie. The Town of Lyons Recycling Center at 198 2nd Avenue takes recyclables. Paint, oil, and batteries never go in a dumpster. The St. Vrain River runs through town, so keep debris out of storm drains and riverbanks. Floodplain rules shape many rebuilds, so check the town floodplain map before a big job.
