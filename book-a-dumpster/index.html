How to book a dumpster rental, step by step

Step one is the size. Five buttons show the five sizes. Tap the one that fits your job. The 20-yard is the most popular pick. If you are torn between two sizes, pick the bigger one. An overfilled box costs more than a slightly bigger box.

Step two is the dates. Pick a delivery date and a rental length. Seven days is standard. Three days suits a fast cleanout. Ten or fourteen days suit a remodel. You can extend later if the job runs long.

Step three is the address. Pick your city from the list. The eight biggest cities are listed by name. Every other Colorado city fits under "Another Colorado city". Then add your street address and tell us where the dumpster goes: driveway, street, jobsite, or somewhere else. Street placement may need a city permit, so say so here and we will flag it.

Step four is your details. Leave your name and phone number. Add a note if the driver needs to know something, like a narrow gate or a steep driveway. Press "Request my booking". That is a request, not a paid order. We call you to confirm the size, the dates, and your exact price. Only then does anything get paid.

What to have ready before you book

Four facts make booking faster. First, your city and street address. Second, your delivery date and how many days you need. Third, your debris type. Heavy debris like concrete, dirt, brick, and tile changes the size you should pick. Fourth, your placement. A driveway needs a clear spot about 60 feet long. A street spot may need a permit.

Not sure about the size? Use the dumpster size calculator. It asks about your job and your debris, then suggests a size. Or read the size guide for the full picture. The Colorado prices page shows published market ranges by size, so you can budget before you book.

What happens after you request a booking

We call you at the number you left. The call confirms four things: the size, the delivery date, the rental length, and your exact price. If the size looks wrong for your debris, we will say so and suggest a better fit. If the date cannot work, we offer the nearest open date.

On delivery day, the driver brings the dumpster to the spot you picked. Keep cars, bins, and low branches clear of the path. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. When the box is full and level, tell us and we pick it up. Do not heap debris above the rim. An overfilled box may need unloading before the truck can haul it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my delivery date after I book?

Yes. Call us as soon as you know the date must move. We move the delivery if the truck schedule allows it. Changes made the day before delivery are the easiest to fit in.

Do I need to be home when the dumpster arrives?

No, you do not need to be home. Mark the spot with cones, boards, or a note on the driveway so the driver places it right. Make sure cars are moved and the path is clear.

What if I need the dumpster longer than I booked?

Call us before the pickup date. Extra days are usually available. The market rate for extra days runs $10 to $25 per day. We confirm the extra cost with you before we extend the rental.

Can I book for a street instead of a driveway?

Yes, but street placement may need a city permit. Denver, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, and Pueblo each have their own permit step. Tell us the placement when you book and we will flag the permit for you.

When do I pay for my dumpster?

You pay nothing on this page. We call you to confirm your size, dates, and exact price first. Payment happens only after that confirmation.
