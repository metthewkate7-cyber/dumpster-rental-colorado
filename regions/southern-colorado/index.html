Dumpster Rental in Southern Colorado

Southern Colorado runs from Colorado Springs and Pueblo in the north to Trinidad and the San Luis Valley in the south. It mixes the second largest city in the state with small mountain towns and farm country.

What Southern Colorado is

Southern Colorado starts at Colorado Springs and runs south to the New Mexico border. Pueblo is the second city. Canon City, Salida, and Buena Vista sit in the Arkansas River valley. Alamosa and Monte Vista sit in the San Luis Valley. Trinidad and Walsenburg sit near the southern border.

Colorado Springs is the hub of the region. It holds about 479,000 people. Pueblo holds about 112,000. The rest of the region is small towns and open land. Jobs range from city remodels to ranch cleanouts to mountain cabin work.

Distances are long here. A haul from Alamosa or Trinidad to the nearest landfill takes real time. Mountain roads add more time in winter. Book early and plan for a wider delivery window outside the two big cities.

Permits and disposal in the south

Colorado Springs street placement needs a revocable permit from Land Use Review at 719-385-5905. Confirm the fee and lead time with the city before you book. Driveway placement needs no permit in most cases.

Pueblo street placement goes through the Pueblo Transportation Division. Confirm the fee and lead time with the city. For the smaller towns, the universal rule applies. Driveway placement needs no permit in most cases. Street placement needs a permit from that town's Public Works office.

Disposal runs through Colorado Springs Landfill and Midway Landfill near Fountain for the north of the region. Pueblo loads go to Southside Landfill about 6.3 miles from downtown. Mountain towns use county transfer stations. Our permit guide has the full city table.

Which size fits southern jobs

The 20 yard box is the southern workhorse. It fits kitchen remodels, multi room cleanouts, and flooring jobs in Colorado Springs and Pueblo. The 10 yard box fits garage cleanouts, small bathrooms, and heavy debris.

Ranch and farm cleanouts need a 30 yard box. Steel mill and construction debris around Pueblo needs a 20 or 30 yard box by weight, not by volume. Mountain cabin jobs often need a 10 yard box because the drive is steep and the road is narrow.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Colorado Springs?

Only for street placement. A street box needs a revocable permit from Land Use Review at 719-385-5905. A driveway box needs no permit in most cases.

What does a dumpster cost in Colorado Springs?

Published ranges put a 10 yard box from about $300 and a 20 yard box near $350 from local operators. Your exact price is confirmed at booking.

Where does Pueblo dumpster debris go?

Most Pueblo loads go to Southside Landfill, about 6.3 miles from downtown Pueblo.

Can I get a dumpster in Alamosa or Trinidad?

Yes. The San Luis Valley and the southern border towns are served. Book early, because the haul is long and the delivery window is wider than in Colorado Springs.

What size fits a mountain cabin cleanout?

Most mountain cabin cleanouts fit a 10 yard box. A small box climbs a steep, narrow drive that a big box cannot use.
