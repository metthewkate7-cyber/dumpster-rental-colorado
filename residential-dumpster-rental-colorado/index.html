What residential dumpster rental colorado covers

Residential rental covers any home job that makes more waste than the trash cart can hold. Cleanouts, remodels, roofing, flooring, yard work, and moving prep all fit. The bin is delivered to your driveway and picked up when you are done.

You load it yourself, on your own schedule. No crew, no hourly rate, no rush. That is the main reason homeowners pick a dumpster over junk removal for big jobs.

Protecting your driveway

A full dumpster is heavy, and the steel rails can mark a driveway. Ask for plywood boards under the rails at booking. Boards spread the weight and stop scratches on concrete and ruts in asphalt.

Boards matter most on new concrete, on hot days when asphalt softens, and on driveways with decorative finishes. The driver can place boards for you if you ask ahead of time.

Keep the bin off the grass if you can. A loaded bin sinks into soft ground after rain. A driveway or a paved pad is the safest spot.

Rules for home dumpster rentals

Check your HOA rules before delivery. Some HOAs limit how long a dumpster can sit in a driveway, or ban street placement. A quick call to the HOA saves a fine.

Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled, and the driver may refuse pickup. Do not load hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, or asbestos.

Tell the driver about low wires, tight gates, and soft ground. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance, plus 25 to 30 feet of vertical clearance.

When Residential Dumpster Rental Is Wrong for You

A home bin is wrong for heavy building debris. Concrete, dirt, and brick hit the weight limit fast. A construction dumpster fits that load better.

It is also wrong for a full estate clear out. A whole house of furniture needs a bigger plan. An estate cleanout dumpster with a 30-yard dumpster fits that job in one trip.

Skip it for a concrete only load too. One small bin of concrete weighs tons. A concrete dumpster in a 10-yard dumpster is the right call.

Frequently asked questions

What size dumpster do I need for a home cleanout?

A single room fits a 10-yard bin. Two or three rooms fit a 15-yard bin. A multi-room cleanout fits a 20-yard bin. A whole-house cleanout needs a 30-yard bin. Use the dumpster size calculator if you are unsure.

How much does a residential dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado ranges run from about $300 for a 10-yard bin in Colorado Springs to $679 for a 20-yard bin on national average. All American Dumpster lists a 20-yard bin at $350 in Colorado Springs. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

Will a dumpster damage my driveway?

It can mark concrete or asphalt if set down bare. Ask for plywood boards under the rails at booking. Boards spread the weight and stop scratches and ruts. Keep the bin off soft grass after rain.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster at my house?

A bin on your own driveway needs no permit. Street placement usually needs a city permit. Denver requires a DOTI street permit with about 5 business days of lead time. Fort Collins charges $25 for a Permit to Obstruct.

What can I not put in a residential dumpster?

No hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, asbestos, or liquids. No refrigerators with freon unless the freon is removed first. Household junk, furniture, wood, drywall, and yard waste are all fine.
