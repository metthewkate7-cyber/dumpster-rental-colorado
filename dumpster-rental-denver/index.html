What size fits your job in Denver?

Denver jobs split three ways: row home remodels, whole-house cleanouts, and construction debris. Row homes take a 20-yard dumpster in most cases. Whole-house cleanouts take a 30-yard dumpster. Construction jobs take a 30-yard or 40-yard dumpster. Tight alleys change the math, so measure first.

  • 10-yard dumpster: small cleanout, one room, small bathroom, or heavy debris like concrete and dirt. About 3 pickup loads. About 50 to 70 bags.
  • 15-yard dumpster: mid-size cleanout, two-room remodel, or flooring through a row home. About 4 to 5 pickup loads. About 80 to 100 bags.
  • 20-yard dumpster: the most rented size in Denver. Kitchen remodel, multi-room cleanout, flooring, or roofing tear-off. About 6 pickup loads. About 110 to 130 bags.
  • 30-yard dumpster: whole-house cleanout, major renovation, or large construction debris. About 9 pickup loads. About 170 to 190 bags. Not for heavy debris.
  • 40-yard dumpster: major demolition or large light bulky debris. About 12 pickup loads. About 230 to 250 bags. Never for concrete, dirt, or brick.

Still picking a size? Use the dumpster size calculator. It asks about your row home remodel or your Central Park cleanout. Then it points to the right size in under a minute. Compare sizes in the full Colorado prices guide too.

Heavy debris needs a small dumpster in Denver too. Concrete, dirt, brick, and tile fill a dumpster by weight long before they fill it by space. A 10-yard dumpster is the right call for a concrete patio tear-out in Berkeley or a dirt job in Sunnyside. A 30-yard dumpster full of concrete would break the weight limit and cost you a big overage fee. Read the weight limits guide before you load heavy material.

Permits are the big Denver difference. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit. A dumpster on a public street, alley, or sidewalk needs a Right-of-Way Revocable Street Occupancy Permit from Denver DOTI, the Department of Transportation and Infrastructure. Call 303-446-3759. Allow about 5 business days. The street permit fee typically runs $75 to $150. Only the hauling company can pull some DOTI permits, so tell us early if you need street placement. How delivery and pickup work in Denver

Start on the booking page. Pick a size first. Then pick your delivery date and rental days. Then add your address and phone. We ring you back to confirm the size, the dates, and the exact price. The booking page takes no payment.

Delivery day is simple, but Denver streets need care. Clear the driveway or street spot before the truck arrives. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. It also needs 25 to 30 feet of clear space above for the lift. Move cars, low branches, and bins out of the way. In a narrow alley, measure the width first and tell us the number. The driver sets boards under the rails, sets the dumpster down, and checks the spot with you.

Load it right and you dodge extra fees. Fill the dumpster level with the top edge. Never fill above it. Put heavy material at the bottom. Put light material on top. Keep paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, and hazardous waste out. Denver runs hazardous waste collection for those items. Never mix concrete with household junk in a big dumpster. Unsure about an item? Check what can go in a dumpster or call us first.

Pickup is one call away. Call or message us when the dumpster is full or your rental days are up. We schedule pickup, usually the next business day. Keep the area around the dumpster clear so the truck can hook it and go. Do not park cars in front of it on pickup day. Street placements need the permit to stay valid until pickup day, so do not let it lapse.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Denver?

You need a permit only when the dumpster sits on a public street, alley, or sidewalk in Denver. The permit is a Right-of-Way Revocable Street Occupancy Permit from Denver DOTI, phone 303-446-3759. It takes about 5 business days. The fee typically runs $75 to $150. A dumpster on your own driveway needs no permit.

How much does a 30-yard dumpster cost in Denver?

Published market prices put a 30-yard dumpster at $535 to $635 in Denver. The source is Dumpster Rental Dogs, checked November 14, 2026. HBS Trash lists a 30-yard dumpster at $345 to $550. Both include 14 days and 3 tons. Your exact price is confirmed when you book.

What size dumpster fits a Denver row home remodel?

A 20-yard dumpster fits most row home remodels in Denver. It holds about 6 pickup loads and 110 to 130 bags. Tight alleys in Capitol Hill and the Highlands may only fit a 10-yard or 15-yard dumpster. Measure your space first and tell us the width.

Can I get same-day dumpster delivery in Denver?

Same-day delivery is possible in Denver when you book early and a truck is free. Most Denver haulers deliver next business day. Book online before midday for the best chance at same-day delivery, and we confirm your window the same day.

Where does Denver dumpster debris go?

Denver debris goes to the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site, known as DADS. It is the main metro landfill for Denver. Hazardous items never go in a dumpster. Paint, chemicals, and batteries need a separate drop-off through Denver city programs.

Will a dumpster fit in a Capitol Hill alley?

Many Capitol Hill alleys are narrow, so a 10-yard or 15-yard dumpster is often the largest size that fits. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance. Measure your alley before you book, and send us the width if you are unsure.

What can I not put in a dumpster in Denver?

You cannot load paint, chemicals, tires, batteries, appliances with freon, or hazardous waste in Denver. Denver runs household hazardous waste collection for those items. Ask us before you load anything odd and we will point you to the right drop-off.
