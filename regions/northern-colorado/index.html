What Northern Colorado is

Northern Colorado is the region north of Denver along the Front Range and out onto the eastern plains. Fort Collins is the largest city. Greeley is the second. Loveland, Longmont, Windsor, and Erie fill the corridor between them. Fort Morgan, Sterling, and Brush sit on the plains to the east.

The job mix is distinct. Colorado State University in Fort Collins and the University of Northern Colorado in Greeley drive student turnover twice a year. Rental cleanouts spike in May and August. Weld County farms and food plants add agricultural and construction debris. New suburbs in Windsor and Erie add construction boxes.

Distances are longer here than in Denver. A haul from Fort Morgan or Sterling to the nearest landfill takes real time. Book early and expect a longer delivery window in the plains towns.

Permits and disposal in the north

Fort Collins has the clearest permit rule in the region. A street box needs a Permit to Obstruct, which costs $25. It comes from the Engineering Department at 281 N College Ave, phone 970-221-6605. Driveway placement needs no permit in most cases.

For Greeley, Loveland, Longmont, and the plains towns, the universal rule applies. Driveway placement needs no permit in most cases. Street placement needs a permit from that city's Public Works office. Check with the city before a street placement.

Disposal runs through Larimer County sites. The Central Diversion and Transfer Station at 2388 W Trilby Rd takes Fort Collins loads. Larimer County North Landfill in Wellington opened in August 2026. Greeley loads use the WM Transfer Station about 2.3 miles from downtown. Greeley also has the North Household Hazardous Waste Facility at 1311 N 17th Ave for paint, chemicals, and batteries.

Which size fits northern jobs

Student turnover jobs need a 10 or 20 yard box. A single apartment cleanout fits a 10 yard box. A multi room rental cleanout fits a 20 yard box. Farm and construction jobs need bigger boxes. A 30 yard box fits a barn cleanout or a large renovation.

Roofing jobs are common after hail season. A one layer tear-off fits a 10 yard box. A two layer tear-off needs a 20 yard box. Shingles are heavy, so weight decides before volume does.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Fort Collins?

Only for street placement. A street box needs a $25 Permit to Obstruct from the Engineering Department at 281 N College Ave. A driveway box needs no permit in most cases.

What does a dumpster cost in Fort Collins?

Published ranges put a 10 yard box near $399 to $499 and a 40 yard box near $699 to $899 for a 5 day term. Your exact price is confirmed at booking.

Where do I take paint and chemicals in Greeley?

The North Household Hazardous Waste Facility at 1311 N 17th Ave in Greeley. Call ahead for hours and accepted items.

Do student move-outs need a dumpster or junk removal?

A dumpster fits a multi room rental cleanout over several days. Junk removal fits a single room that is already sorted. Most May and August turnovers use a 10 or 20 yard box.

Can I get a dumpster in Fort Morgan or Sterling?

Yes. Plains towns are served, but the haul is longer. Book early and expect a wider delivery window than in Fort Collins or Greeley.
