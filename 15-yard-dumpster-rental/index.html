15 Yard Dumpster Rental in Colorado

A 15 yard dumpster rental sits between the small 10-yard bin and the popular 20-yard bin. It suits mid-size cleanouts and two-room remodels across Colorado.

Quick answer

A 15-yard dumpster holds about 4 to 5 pickup loads, or 80 to 100 trash bags. It measures about 16 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 4 feet high. It suits a mid-size cleanout, a two-room remodel, or a flooring job. Published Colorado prices for this size run $300 to $750, with a typical local price of $375 to $520.

What a 15 yard dumpster rental holds

A 15-yard bin holds about 80 to 100 trash bags. That is 4 to 5 pickup truck loads. It is the middle step for jobs that are too big for a 10-yard bin but do not need a 20-yard bin.

The extra 5 yards over the small bin make a real difference. You get room for a second room of debris, or a full flooring job plus the old furniture. Many renters pick this size when they are unsure, because it covers the gap.

It also works for mixed loads. Household junk, wood, drywall, and old carpet all fit together. Just keep heavy material low in the bin and light waste on top.

At 16 feet long, the bin fits a standard two-car driveway. The truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance to drop it. The low 4-foot walls make loading easy by hand.

Weight allowance and overage fees

Most 15-yard rentals include 1.5 to 3 tons of debris. That covers household junk and light building waste with room to spare. Heavy loads need care. Shingles, tile, and plaster weigh far more than they look.

Overage fees in Colorado typically run $40 to $100 per ton. Mo's Hauling in Denver charges $70 per ton over the limit. HBS Trash charges $75 per ton. Keep the load level with the top rail. An overfilled bin cannot be hauled safely and may draw an extra fee.

Wet waste weighs more than dry waste. Cover the bin with a tarp if rain is forecast. A tarp also keeps light debris from blowing out on a windy Colorado day.

Best jobs for a 15-yard dumpster

  • Mid-size cleanout of two or three rooms
  • Two-room remodel, bath plus bedroom
  • Full flooring removal in a mid-size home
  • Basement cleanout with old furniture and boxes
  • Roofing tear-off on a mid-size roof
  • Deck removal plus yard waste
  • Garage cleanout with tools, boxes, and old gear

If the job grows past two rooms, step up to the 20-yard dumpster. It is the most rented size in Colorado for a reason.

When a 15-yard dumpster is the wrong size

A 15-yard bin is wrong for a whole-house cleanout. Three or more rooms of furniture and junk will fill it fast. You would pay for a second haul. A 30-yard dumpster handles a full house in one trip.

It is also wrong for a full load of concrete or dirt. Heavy material hits the weight limit long before the bin looks full. Use a 10-yard dumpster for heavy debris, and fill it only part way.

Skip this size for major demolition too. Torn-out walls, joists, and roofing from a big demo need a taller bin. The 30-yard and 40-yard dumpster sizes fit demolition waste better.

Where a 15-yard dumpster fits

A 15-yard bin fits a standard driveway in nearly every Colorado home. At 16 feet long, it leaves room for a car beside it on a two-car drive. It also fits in a side yard if the truck can reach it.

Ask for plywood boards under the rails. Boards protect concrete and asphalt from scratches and dents. They matter most on new driveways and on hot days, when asphalt softens.

Street placement may need a city permit. Denver requires a Right-of-Way permit from DOTI, with about 5 business days of lead time. Fort Collins charges $25 for a Permit to Obstruct. Check the Colorado permit guide for your city before you book.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 15-yard dumpster cost in Colorado?

Published Colorado prices for a 15-yard bin run $300 to $750. A typical local price is $375 to $520. Mo's Hauling in Denver lists $300 for 3 days and $365 for 7 days. The national average is near $600, per Dumpsters.com. Our exact price is confirmed when you book.

How many trash bags fit in a 15-yard dumpster?

About 80 to 100 standard trash bags fit in a 15-yard dumpster. That is about 4 to 5 pickup truck loads. Large bulky items reduce the bag count, so break down furniture when you can.

Is a 15-yard dumpster big enough for a kitchen remodel?

It depends on the kitchen. A small kitchen remodel fits in a 15-yard bin. A full kitchen with cabinets, counters, flooring, and appliances usually needs a 20-yard bin. Tell us the scope when you book and we will confirm the size.

What is the weight limit on a 15-yard dumpster?

Most 15-yard rentals include 1.5 to 3 tons. Household junk rarely hits the limit. Roofing shingles, tile, plaster, and concrete can hit it fast. Overage in Colorado typically runs $40 to $100 per ton.

Will a 15-yard dumpster fit in my driveway?

Yes, in most cases. The bin is about 16 feet long and 8 feet wide. The delivery truck needs about 10.5 feet of width and 60 feet of straight clearance to set it down safely.

How long can I keep a 15-yard dumpster?

A standard rental runs 7 to 14 days, and 7 days is the most common term. Extra days typically cost $10 to $25 per day. Choose your rental length at booking and extend early if the job runs long.
